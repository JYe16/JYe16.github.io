I am a Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at Florida State University, advised by Dr. Xin Liu. My research lies at the intersection of trustworthy AI, wireless/RF sensing, edge intelligence, and secure large language model deployment. I develop privacy-preserving and resource-efficient AI systems that enable machines to perceive, understand, and protect human-centered physical environments.
